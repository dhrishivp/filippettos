Italian delicatessen · Little Chalfont

Proper coffee, warm panini and gelato on Chenies Parade.

A small Italian deli and café by Chalfont & Latimer station. Come in for a cappuccino and a slice of almond cake, stay for lunch, and leave with a cone.

Opening hours Tuesday to Saturday from 8am

From the counter

What's on changes with the day and the season. Here's what people come back for.

Coffee & cake

Espresso, cappuccino and lattes made the Italian way, with almond cake and other bakes alongside.

Panini

Pressed to order and filled with Italian cured meats, cheeses and grilled vegetables.

Pastries

Flaky sfogliatelle filled with cream, along with a changing selection of Italian cakes and sweets.

Gelato

Scoops in a cone or a tub, from pistachio and chocolate to fruit ripples.
